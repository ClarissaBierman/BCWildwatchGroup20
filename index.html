How to Report Animals
  1. Go to the Report Incident tab
  2. Select the animal type and your campus/zone
  3. Describe what you saw and use "Locate Me" for GPS
  4. Click Submit — security will be notified immediately
Safety Tips

Snakes: Keep your distance, do not provoke. Alert security immediately.

Bees: Move away slowly, do not swat. Cover your face if swarming.

Stray animals: Do not approach or feed. Report to security.

Cockroaches/Pests: Note the location and report. Do not disturb.

Spiders: Keep distance, check shoes and clothing in affected areas.

What Happens Next?

Once you submit a report, our security team will be notified and will dispatch a trained responder to assess the situation.

For non-emergencies, please allow up to 30 minutes – 1 hour for a response during business hours.

The responder will safely handle the animal, which may involve relocation, contacting professional wildlife services, or providing aid.
